How this works

1. Technical analysis
Moving averages, RSI, MACD, Bollinger Bands, ATR and swing-based support/resistance, computed from your OHLCV data.
2. Trend detection
Price-vs-MA ordering classifies uptrend / downtrend / sideways; a 60-day linear regression R² and t-stat cross-check the strength.
3. Analytic probability
A closed-form Brownian-motion-with-drift barrier formula estimates the odds of hitting the target before the stop.
4. Monte Carlo simulation
Thousands of simulated GBM price paths under your chosen horizon cross-check the analytic estimate, with a 95% confidence interval.
5. Walk-forward backtest
The exact same rule is replayed across your historical bars to get a real, data-driven win rate — not just a model.
6. Blend, rank & size
The four probability sources are blended by precision, scenarios are ranked by expected value, and position size follows from your risk budget and a Kelly cross-check.
Educational tool only — not financial advice. Every probability here comes from a model (Brownian-motion assumptions, historical volatility, a finite backtest sample) applied to historical price data; none of it predicts the future with certainty, and small samples produce wide confidence intervals. Past performance does not guarantee future results. Always use a stop loss, size positions with real risk management, and do your own research before risking real capital.
